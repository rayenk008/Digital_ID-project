signature verification

A signed attestation is a statement confirming that a specific person has genuinely signed a document and agrees with its contents. It’s used in official contexts (like municipal services) to verify identity and prove the signature is real.

Certified true copy

certified true copy of an original document—verified to match the original exactly (text, signatures, stamps, etc.). It’s used when a copy must be accepted in place of the original

Treasury

The treasury (cashier) office handles official payments (fees, taxes, fines), issues formal receipts, manages incoming funds, and records all financial transactions for the department.

CNSS

CNRPS

CNRPS is Tunisia’s National Pension and Social Welfare Fund. It manages retirement pensions and social protection benefits for public-sector employees, including retirement, disability, and survivors’ benefits.
